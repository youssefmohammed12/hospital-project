01 / THE PROJECT

Built to understand how a complex application fits together.

The goal was to build more than a collection of disconnected screens. HealthBridge brings patient booking, clinician workflows and hospital administration into one role-aware application, with shared information and clear boundaries between responsibilities.

Patients can find a doctor, book through a four-step wizard with real-time availability checks, reschedule appointments, review their records and prescriptions, and leave ratings after appointments.

Clinicians can configure weekly availability, slot intervals, breaks and leave; manage appointment queues; document visits with SOAP encounter notes and EMR workflows; and prepare prescriptions in the digital prescription studio.

Administrators get analytics for patient volume, revenue estimates, department load and completion rates, alongside staff and department management, an audit trail, and a support-message inbox for patient inquiries and replies.

TECHNOLOGY FOUNDATION

PHP 8+ / OOP PDO MariaDB Vanilla JavaScript ES6+ Modular CSS3 No frontend framework

Server-rendered PHP pages use PDO for database access; browser interactions are built with vanilla ES6+ JavaScript and modular CSS3, without a frontend framework.

02 / WHAT GUIDES THE WORK

A few principles shape every part of the experience.

These are engineering goals for the demonstration—not claims about a real healthcare provider.

01

Clear journeys

Keep navigation and task steps understandable, from exploring the doctor directory to reviewing an appointment.

02

Role-aware access

Organize patient, clinician and administrator tools around distinct responsibilities and access boundaries.

03

Maintainable engineering

Favor consistent patterns, focused modules and predictable behavior over disconnected one-off solutions.

IMPORTANT PROJECT SCOPE

A software demonstration—not a healthcare provider.

HealthBridge is a student portfolio project. It does not provide medical care, medical advice, emergency assistance or real-world appointments. All sample records are synthetic. Do not enter real personal, account or health information.
